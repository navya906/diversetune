How It Works

Five Methods, One Question

Does re-ranking buy diversity, and is the DPP kernel needed, or is its niche floor doing the work?

Greedy

Popularity-Based

Ranks all songs by popularity (ties broken by track id) and returns the top K=10, excluding the seed songs. The "Top 50 radio" baseline.

Low diversityMainstream onlyFast
Time: O(n log n) Space: O(n)

Content Filtering

Cosine similarity

Scores every non-seed track by mean cosine similarity to the liked songs and returns the top K=10. Relevant, but candidates tend to look alike.

Low diversityRelevant
Time: O(n·m·d + n log n) Space: O(n)

MMR (λ=0.7)

Maximal Marginal Relevance

Takes the top N=50 similar tracks, then greedily picks the one maximising λ·relevance − (1−λ)·max similarity to already-picked tracks (Carbonell & Goldstein, 1998). No popularity constraint.

Redundancy penaltySimple
Time: O(n·m·d + N·K·d) Space: O(N)

MMR + niche floor

MMR with fairness constraint

The same MMR selection, followed by the identical ≥20% niche-track floor (popularity < 40) that DPP uses. Isolates the effect of the constraint from the DPP kernel.

Niche floorAblation
Time: O(n·m·d + N·K·d) Space: O(N)

Graph DPP Rerank

Similarity + DPP re-ranking

Takes the top N=50 similar tracks, greedily maximises the log-determinant of a quality-weighted similarity kernel, then enforces the same ≥20% niche floor.

Diversity-awareNiche floorCostly
Time: O(N²·d + N·K⁴) Space: O(N²)

Findings

Conclusions & Key Takeaways

🔬

Greedy is the low-diversity baseline

Greedy's lists are essentially all the same genre at the top of the popularity range, with no niche songs at all. Its low diversity is genuine: it holds once the popularity ceiling and the arbitrary tie-breaking of the earlier pipeline are fixed.

💡

Re-ranking helps, modestly

MMR, MMR + floor and DPP all raise ILD above plain Content Filtering, and DPP is highest. These ILD differences are the only ones among non-Greedy methods that survive a Bonferroni correction. Most lists from every method are still close to single-genre, so the absolute gains are small.

⚖️

The niche floor does the fairness work

Adding the same niche floor to MMR reproduces DPP's niche representation and floor-compliance, without a detectable cost to ILD. DPP's kernel still buys a small, statistically clear ILD edge over MMR + floor. No niche-% difference between the methods is significant at n=30.

⚠️

Caveats

Genres are playlist-level labels (noisy), seed sets are random tracks rather than real listening histories, and similarity ignores audio features. See repo_analysis.md for the full statistics and limitations.

📐 Metrics Formulas

Intra-List Diversity (ILD)

ILD = 1|L|(|L|−1) ∑i≠j (1 − sim(i,j))

Where sim(i,j) = cosine similarity between feature vectors

Gini index (popularity concentration)

G = ∑i (2i − n − 1) · xin · ∑ xi

Applied to popularity distribution of recommended songs

DPP Kernel

Lij = qi · qj · Sij

Quality-weighted similarity kernel for determinantal point process

MMR

argmaxdi ∈ R∖S [ λ·Sim(di, seed) − (1−λ)·maxdj ∈ S Sim(di, dj) ]

Greedy selection; Sim is cosine on the same feature vectors as DPP
